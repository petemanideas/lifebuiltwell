Simple Healthy Breakfast Ideas

Breakfast doesn't need to be clever. It needs to be easy enough to make on a busy morning and filling enough to carry you through to lunch. These are the kinds of breakfasts I actually eat: a bowl, some fruit, a handful of nuts and a warm drink. Nothing fancy, just steady.

The breakfast formula 10 easy ideas
Keep it simple

The 4-part breakfast formula

Just like the simple plate I use for lunch and dinner, breakfast works best when it has a few basic building blocks. Pick one from each and you're done.

  1. Protein: Greek yoghurt, eggs, milk, cottage cheese, or nut butter on toast. This is what keeps you full.
  2. Fibre-rich carbs: oats, wholegrain cereal or muesli, or wholegrain or sourdough toast.
  3. Fruit: berries, orange slices, grapes, a banana. Whatever is in season and on hand.
  4. Healthy fats: a small handful of nuts, seeds, avocado or nut butter.

Add water and a warm drink, and you have a breakfast that sets the day up well.

Mix and match

10 easy healthy breakfast ideas

  1. Yoghurt bowl: Greek yoghurt, muesli, berries and a sprinkle of nuts.
  2. Overnight oats: oats soaked in milk overnight, topped with banana and cinnamon.
  3. Toast and nut butter: wholegrain toast, peanut butter, sliced banana or berries.
  4. Eggs on toast: two eggs any style, with tomato or spinach on the side.
  5. Cereal plus a fruit plate: a wholegrain cereal with milk, plus orange slices and a handful of nuts.
  6. Porridge: warm oats with grated apple, cinnamon and walnuts. Perfect for winter.
  7. Avocado toast: smashed avocado, a squeeze of lemon, and an egg or some feta.
  8. Cottage cheese and fruit: cottage cheese with pineapple or berries and a few seeds.
  9. Breakfast smoothie: milk or yoghurt, frozen berries, a banana and a spoon of oats.
  10. Leftovers: there's no rule against last night's dinner. Protein and veg in the morning works well.
Make it stick

Tips for keeping breakfast simple

  • Keep a short rotation. Three or four go-to breakfasts remove the decision-making.
  • Set up the night before. Bowls out, oats soaking, fruit washed.
  • Use a tray or a plate. Sitting down to a proper breakfast makes you slow down and actually enjoy it.
  • Don't chase perfection. A decent breakfast most days beats a perfect one once a week.
  • Pair it with a quiet moment. Breakfast is a natural time for a short devotion or some gratitude before the day gets busy.
